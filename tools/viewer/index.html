Joint calibration (gear button ⚙)

Per-arm calibration: urdf = sign × value + offset per joint, per side. Use this when one arm's encoders are biased relative to URDF zero.

Fast workflow: pause on a frame where you know the arm is at its physical home pose, then click "Snap current → zero" for that arm. The offsets are auto-set so the URDF renders that frame as home, and motion is preserved relative to it.

Sign toggle flips axis convention, offset slider shifts the zero point. Reset per-arm restores identity. Settings persist in localStorage.
